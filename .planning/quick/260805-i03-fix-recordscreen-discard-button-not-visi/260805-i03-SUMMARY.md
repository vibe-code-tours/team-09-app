# 260805-i03 — Fix RecordScreen Discard Button Not Visible on Small Screens

## Objective

Fix RecordScreen bottom section overflow on small screens (e.g. Mi 6) so the Discard button is always reachable during and after transcription.

## What Was Done

Wrapped the three child Views inside the `bottomSection` Animated.View (playbackContainer, statusContainer, actionContainer) in a `ScrollView` component. This ensures the Discard button is always reachable by scrolling on screens with limited height.

### Changes Made to `src/screens/RecordScreen.tsx`

1. Added `ScrollView` to the import list from `react-native`.
2. Wrapped playbackContainer, statusContainer, and actionContainer inside a `ScrollView` with `style={{ flex: 1 }}`, `contentContainerStyle={styles.scrollContent}`, `showsVerticalScrollIndicator={false}`, and `bounces={false}`.
3. Moved `paddingTop: 20` from `styles.bottomSection` into the ScrollView's `contentContainerStyle` via a new `styles.scrollContent` entry with `paddingTop: 20` and `paddingBottom: 20`.
4. `styles.bottomSection` now only has `flex: 1` and `alignItems: 'center'`.

No logic, state, handlers, or functionality was changed.

## Verification

- TypeScript (`npx tsc --noEmit`): passes with no errors in RecordScreen.tsx
- ScrollView wraps all bottomSection children with clean scroll behavior
- No bounce, no scroll indicator
- Layout unchanged on normal-size screens
