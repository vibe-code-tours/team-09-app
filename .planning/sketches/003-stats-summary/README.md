# Sketch 003: Stats & Summary

## Design Question
How should the daily/weekly stats and summary section look on the home screen?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/003-stats-summary/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants

- **A: Metric Cards Row** — Simple three-card row showing Today/This Week/Total. Clean, scannable, minimal. One card highlighted as primary metric.

- **B: Summary Banner** — Rich banner with streak badge, multiple stats, progress bar, and motivational text. More engaging, gamification feel.

- **C: Category Grid** — 3x2 grid showing entries per category this week. Category colors as top borders. Gives instant breakdown of what you've been logging.

## What to Look For

1. **Motivation** — Which variant makes you want to record more?
2. **Information value** — Which stats are most useful at a glance?
3. **Space efficiency** — How much home screen real estate does it consume?
4. **Visual interest** — Which feels more alive and engaging?
5. **Category awareness** — Does it help you see patterns in your recording habits?
