# Sketch 004: Money Overview

## Design Question
How should the Money tab's overview/dashboard section feel? What metrics and layout give users a useful snapshot of their spending?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/004-money-overview/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants

- **A: Cash Overview** — Cash App-style prominent total with green hero banner. Quick stats row (today, avg/day, entries) + mini bar chart + recent expenses list. Bold, metric-heavy, visual.

- **B: Category Breakdown** — Total at top, then 2x2 grid of spending categories (Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills). Each card has icon, amount, and entry count. Below that, a recent list with category color dots. Structured, analytical.

- **C: Minimal Feed** — Compact total inline with trend indicator ("↓ 12% vs last month"). Expenses grouped by day with daily subtotals. Clean, scannable, information-dense. No charts.

## What to Look For

1. **Glanceability** — How quickly can you see "how much have I spent?"
2. **Insight depth** — Do the metrics help you understand spending patterns?
3. **Information density** — Too much or too little on screen at once?
4. **Consistency** — Which variant feels most like the existing HomeScreen sketches?
5. **Mobile ergonomics** — Is everything reachable and readable on a phone?

## States to Test
Use the bottom-left states bar to switch between:
- **Populated** — Multiple expense entries visible
- **Empty** — No money entries yet
